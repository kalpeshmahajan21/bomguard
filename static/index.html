        How a part gets checked

        1. CleanNemotron Nano reads any BOM export, fixes manufacturer names and flags generic passives so no credits are wasted on a 10k resistor.
        2. SearchTavily searches manufacturer pages, distributors and notices for each part's lifecycle, stock and RoHS status.
        3. ExtractNemotron Super turns those pages into facts. A claim without a retrieved source is thrown away, and "Unknown" is an allowed answer.
        4. ReplaceFor risky parts, a second search finds successors. Nemotron Ultra judges each one as drop-in, pin-compatible or a PCB change.
        5. BriefUltra reads recent supply-chain news for your riskiest vendors and writes what to fix first.

        The risk score is plain arithmetic on the extracted facts, not a model's opinion, so you can see why a part scored what it did.
